Methodology

  • Sportsbook odds → implied probability per book, de-vigged by dividing each side by the overround (basic normalization). Consensus = mean de-vigged home-win probability across books.
  • Polymarket: Yes-token price on the game moneyline market. Kalshi: orderbook mid.
  • Team matching is conservative — a game only appears when both teams resolve through the canonical table; anything ambiguous is listed as unmatched, never guessed.
  • Context: ESPN records/injuries + Open-Meteo weather for outdoor NFL games. Free sources only.
