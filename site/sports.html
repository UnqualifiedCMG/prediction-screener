Sports: markets vs books

Methodology

  • Moneyline: sportsbook odds → implied probability per book, de-vigged by dividing each side by the overround (basic normalization). Consensus = mean de-vigged home-win probability across books.
  • Spread: mean of each book's home-team point spread. Books sitting ≥ 1.0 point off the cross-book average are flagged — a book disagreeing with the market on the number.
  • Total: mean over/under line across books.
  • Polymarket: Yes-token price on the game moneyline market. (Kalshi support is parked — its sports data is too thin to be useful right now.)
  • Team matching is conservative — a game only appears when both teams resolve through the canonical table; anything ambiguous is listed as unmatched, never guessed.
  • Context: ESPN records/injuries + Open-Meteo weather for outdoor NFL games. Free sources only.
