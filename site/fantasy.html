The model (no black boxes)

  • NFL projected PPR = 0.65 × last-5-game PPR average + 0.35 × season PPR average, then: +(game_total − 44) × 0.10 for game environment.
  • Blowout adjustments (from the sportsbook spread): team favored by 7+ → RB ×1.08, QB ×0.95. Team underdog by 7+ → WR/TE ×1.05.
  • Usage bump: ×1.12 when a teammate at the same position is listed Out/Doubtful on the ESPN injury report.
  • Signals show the inputs: game total, spread, Polymarket game price, and which adjustments fired — so you can audit every number.
  • NBA: 2025-26 per-game fantasy averages (pts + 1.2·reb + 1.5·ast + 3·stl + 3·blk_stl; Sleeper bundles blocks+steals) as a preseason baseline. Not a projection.
  • MLB: final 2026 actuals only. No model, no projections.

Limitations

  • Without an Odds API key, total/spread adjustments are skipped and the projection is just form-based — the page says so per player.
  • Last-5 averages overweight recent hot streaks; rookies and returning injured players have thin samples.
  • ESPN injury reports lag real news by hours. Verify before acting.
  • Sleeper's weekly stats can revise after stat corrections.
