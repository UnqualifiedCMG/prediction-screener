About & methodology

PolyScope is a research snapshot of prediction-market data — Polymarket and Kalshi — rebuilt on a schedule. It tracks what open markets are priced at, where the volume is, and how extreme prices have resolved historically. It is built for research. It is not financial advice and nothing here is a recommendation to trade.

Data sources

  • Polymarket — public gamma API (gamma-api.polymarket.com/events) for market listings, plus the CLOB API (clob.polymarket.com/prices-history) for daily price history. Prices shown are Yes-token prices as probabilities.
  • Kalshi — public trade API (api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets) for open markets. Prices shown are the bid/ask midpoint, not last-trade prices, because Kalshi does not expose last-trade prices on this endpoint.

What the screens mean

  • Top volume — most liquid open markets. Liquidity means a position can actually be entered and exited; volume is also a proxy for attention.
  • Biggest 24h movers — largest price moves in the last day, filtered to markets with real 24h volume so thin books don't dominate. Polymarket only.
  • Closing soon — markets expiring within 7 days, sorted by volume. Mispriced tails tend to surface near deadlines.
  • Extremes watch — markets priced ≥90% or ≤10% with high 24h volume. Crowded consensus trades: useful both as signals and as things to argue against.
  • Fresh listings — markets created in the last 48h; new narratives before the crowd has priced them.

How the backtest works

  • Pulls resolved Polymarket markets (gamma API, closed=true), newest first, across end-date windows to get past the API's pagination cap.
  • Resolution comes from each market's final outcomePrices (1.00/0.00). Markets without a clean resolution (voided, ambiguous) are skipped and counted, never guessed.
  • Each market's daily price history is walked to find its max/min price, final-week price, and first touch of 90¢/10¢.
  • Multi-outcome markets are included as per-outcome binary records — only when exactly one outcome has a final price above 99¢ (a clean winner).
  • Every bucket reports its N. Small-N buckets are noise, not signal.

Known limitations

  • Kalshi has no price-history API, so "reached 90¢ at some point" is uncomputable there, and Kalshi never appears in the movers screen. Kalshi's markets endpoint is also flooded with provisional sports-combo scaffolds; the screener requests markets closing 14+ days out and drops provisional/multivariate records, which means near-term Kalshi markets don't appear.
  • Daily price samples can miss intraday spikes.
  • Only markets the APIs still list are analyzed — deleted or voided markets are excluded, which can inflate historical hit rates (selection bias).
  • Averages are unconditional across categories (sports, politics, crypto). Base rates differ wildly by category.
  • Polymarket volume24h is event-level, shared across that event's markets; per-market 24h volume isn't exposed.
  • Stale records (close date passed but still listed open) are filtered out.
