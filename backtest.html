📊 Backtest: do 90¢ markets resolve YES?

Generated 2026-10-18 23:54 UTC · 1490 resolved Polymarket binary markets · min volume $1,000 · daily price samples · skipped 10430 markets, 10 lacked price history
Key insight — read this first. 90¢ is typically first touched only 2.4 hours before resolution — i.e. after the outcome is effectively decided (a goal scored, a vote counted). The 96%+ hit rate is mostly hindsight, not a tradeable edge: by the time a market prints 90¢, the information is already public. The real edge is getting in before the crowd moves it there.

The headline stat

Markets that reached ≥90¢ resolved YES 92.3% of the time (n=736).

But that includes markets that later collapsed. Split by final-week price:

  • Reached ≥90¢ and still ≥80¢ in the final week → YES 100.0% (n=677)
  • Reached ≥90¢ but fell below 80¢ by the final week → YES 3.4% (n=59)

Read this before trading on it
  • Daily price samples can miss intraday spikes — a market that briefly touched 90c between samples is counted as never reaching it.
  • 'Reached 90c' includes markets that later collapsed; see the collapse split above. Past crowd confidence is not future accuracy.
  • Selection bias: only markets the gamma API still lists. Deleted, voided, or ambiguous markets are excluded, which can inflate hit rates.
  • N per bucket is shown for a reason — buckets with small N are noise, not signal. Treat anything under ~30 as anecdote.
  • These are unconditional averages across all categories (sports, politics, crypto). Base rates differ wildly by category.
  • Polymarket only. Kalshi has no price-history API, so its markets can't be analyzed this way.
